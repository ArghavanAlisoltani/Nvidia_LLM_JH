📚 For beginner bioinformatics students | ⏱️ ~10 minutes

What Is a Protein?

Proteins are the workhorses of the cell — they catalyze reactions, transport molecules, provide structure, and much more. Every protein is a chain of amino acids (also called residues).

There are 20 standard amino acids, each with a unique chemical property. Think of them as an alphabet of 20 letters.

Why Sequences Matter

  • Sequence determines structure: The order of amino acids dictates how the protein folds into its 3D shape.
  • Structure determines function: The 3D shape determines what the protein does.
  • Evolution conserves sequences: Similar sequences often mean similar functions across species.
Key insight: Just as the order of letters determines the meaning of a word, the order of amino acids determines the function of a protein.
